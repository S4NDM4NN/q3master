Ignored Hosts

Manipulating your listing on this master — registering the same server under many fake ports/addresses to inflate your apparent presence, spoofing player counts, or similar — gets an IP permanently ignored: every port on it is excluded from the server list and network totals, and it's named here. This is checked automatically — the tool watches for servers reporting identical hostname, map, and player data under different addresses and blocks them on its own, no manual reporting required.
